# Art direction used for this study

Built-in image generation produced the three PNG references in this directory.
These are manually authored art directions, not image metadata.

## Cottage variant

Related cottage in the same medieval village as cottage-original.png. Single
isolated complete 3D asset concept, three-quarter view with front door and right
side visible, solid magenta background. Match detailed hand-painted game art:
warm aged oak timber, cream lime plaster, grey mossy slate, block stone foundation.
Wider lower single-storey craftsman's cottage, asymmetrical roof with a small
front-facing dormer, entrance offset left under a supported porch canopy, broad
shuttered front windows, chunky side chimney. No surrounding terrain, text, or
cutoffs. Different silhouette with the same materials and human-scale openings.

## Carpenter

Full-body adult male medieval village carpenter, neutral friendly face, short
brown hair, olive linen shirt with elbow-length sleeves, brown leather belt,
charcoal brown trousers and simple brown boots. Accurate adult anatomy, clear
fingers, no held items. Straight front-view T-pose with arms horizontal, palms
down and feet apart. Soft neutral light, painterly material detail, light grey
background, complete silhouette with margins. Intended for automatic rigging.

## Terrain atlas

Three equal square panels horizontally, no borders or labels. Straight-down
seamless diffuse albedo materials without shadows or perspective. Olive meadow
turf with fine short grass and clover; warm grey-brown compacted footpath soil
with tiny embedded stones; dark moist soil with small oak leaves, roots and moss.
Restrained natural hand-painted medieval game palette and fine-scale detail.
Panels cropped separately with four pixels of inset to avoid boundary bleed.
